Common Types of Medical Malpractice Claims We Handle

As experienced medical malpractice lawyers, we handle the full spectrum of medical malpractice cases. These mistakes can occur in hospitals, clinics, emergency rooms, surgical centers, and private practices across Florida.

Type of Malpractice Description Devastating Consequences
Diagnostic Errors Misdiagnosis, Delayed Diagnosis, or Failure to Diagnose. The physician fails to correctly identify a condition, or delays diagnosis, leading to a patient missing critical, timely treatment. Cancer spreading, stroke damage becoming irreversible, or heart attack treatment being delayed.
Surgical Errors Mistakes made before, during, or after a procedure. Includes wrong-site surgery, wrong-patient surgery, or performing the wrong procedure. Foreign object left behind (e.g., sponges, instruments), nerve damage, accidental organ perforation, or serious infections.
Birth Injuries Negligence before, during, or immediately after delivery resulting in harm to the mother or child. Cerebral Palsy or other permanent neurological damage from oxygen deprivation, Brachial Plexus injuries from improper delivery techniques, or maternal hemorrhaging.
Medication Errors Prescribing the wrong drug, incorrect dosage, administering medication to the wrong patient, or failing to check for dangerous drug interactions/allergies. Allergic reactions, organ failure, brain damage, or wrongful death.
Anesthesia Errors Mistakes by an anesthesiologist, including administering the wrong type or amount of anesthetic, or failing to properly monitor the patient’s vital signs during surgery. Permanent brain damage, stroke, cardiac arrest, or death.
Failure to Treat Making a correct diagnosis but failing to provide appropriate or timely follow-up care, failing to refer to a specialist, or premature discharge. Undiagnosed internal bleeding, untreated infection leading to sepsis, or allowing a condition to worsen.

The Full Scope of Damages We Seek

Medical malpractice injuries often result in permanent, life-altering consequences, requiring specialized and lifelong care. We work with life-care planners, medical experts, and economists to calculate the full spectrum of your losses, ensuring you are compensated for:

Current and Future Medical Expenses

This includes emergency care, surgeries, hospital stays, prescription medication, physical therapy, long-term nursing care, and necessary adaptive equipment (wheelchairs, home modifications).

Lost Wages and Loss of Earning Capacity

Compensation for income lost today and all future income the injury prevents you from earning.

Pain and Suffering

Damages for the physical pain, emotional distress, mental anguish, and humiliation caused by the injury.

Loss of Consortium

Compensation for the victim’s spouse for the loss of companionship, support, and affection.

Wrongful Death

If malpractice resulted in the death of a loved one, we pursue a wrongful death claim to recover funeral expenses, loss of financial support, and pain and suffering of the surviving family members.
