Types of Florida Real Estate Law We Handle

The field of real estate law is broad, covering everything from simple residential closings to multifaceted commercial development projects. The attorneys at Lieberman Chamberlain PLLC have extensive experience across the full spectrum of real estate matters. As your dedicated real estate lawyer, we provide skilled representation in numerous areas.

Our services cover:

Commercial and Residential Leases

Drafting, reviewing, and negotiating lease agreements to protect the rights of landlords and tenants. This includes handling commercial evictions.

Eminent Domain

When the government seeks to take private property for public use, it is known as eminent domain. While property owners are entitled to “just compensation,” the initial offer from the government may not reflect the true value of your property. We help property owners navigate this complex process, ensuring their rights are upheld and they receive fair compensation for their land.

Construction Litigation

Construction projects involve numerous parties, including contractors, subcontractors, architects, and owners. Disputes can arise over project delays, budget overruns, defective workmanship, or payment issues. Our firm represents clients in all types of construction litigation, working to resolve conflicts efficiently and protect your financial investment.

Title Disputes

A clear title is essential for any real estate transaction. Title disputes can emerge from undisclosed liens, boundary disagreements, errors in public records, or claims from previous owners. A real estate attorney from our firm can conduct thorough title searches, identify potential issues, and take legal action to resolve title defects, securing your ownership rights through quiet title actions when necessary.

Zoning Laws

Navigating local zoning ordinances and land use regulations is a critical part of real estate development. We assist developers, builders, and property owners in understanding and complying with Jacksonville’s complex zoning laws. Our services include applying for variances, special use permits, and rezoning, helping you move your project forward while adhering to all legal requirements.

Foreclosures

Facing foreclosure can be an incredibly stressful experience for a homeowner, while for a lender, it is a necessary process to recover a defaulted loan. We provide representation for both borrowers and lenders in foreclosure proceedings. We can help homeowners explore alternatives like loan modifications or short sales, and we assist lenders in navigating the legal process to reclaim their assets in a compliant manner.

Partition Actions

When co-owners of a property cannot agree on its use or disposal, a partition action may be necessary. This legal process allows the court to either physically divide the property or order its sale and distribute the proceeds among the owners. We represent co-owners in partition actions to ensure their interests are fairly represented and the matter is resolved equitably.

Real Estate Contracts

Contracts are the cornerstone of nearly every real estate transaction. Our team is skilled in managing all forms of real estate contracts to protect your legal and financial interests.

  • Land Sale Contracts: We draft and review contracts for the sale of undeveloped land, addressing unique considerations like zoning, environmental assessments, and access rights.
  • Real Estate Purchase Contracts: For residential and commercial properties, we ensure purchase agreements are comprehensive, covering contingencies, financing, inspections, and closing details to prevent future complications.
  • Contract Disputes: When a party fails to uphold its contractual obligations, a dispute arises. We provide strong representation in breach of contract cases, seeking remedies such as specific performance or monetary damages.

Real Estate Disputes and Litigation in Jacksonville FL

Common disputes we handle include:

  • Breach of Contract: One party fails to meet their obligations under a purchase agreement or lease.
  • Boundary and Easement Disputes: Disagreements between neighbors over property lines or rights of way.
  • Landlord-Tenant Conflicts: Issues related to unpaid rent, property damage, or unlawful evictions.
  • Construction Defects: Claims against builders or contractors for subpar workmanship.
  • Title Claims: Challenges to your ownership of a property from third parties.

As your commercial real estate attorney in Jacksonville, FL, we understand the high stakes involved in business-related property disputes. We work diligently to achieve a resolution that aligns with your business objectives, whether that involves enforcing a contract or defending against a claim. Our approach is both strategic and assertive, aimed at protecting your assets and minimizing disruption to your operations.

Do I need a real estate attorney in Jacksonville FL?

While not every real estate transaction in Florida legally requires an attorney, engaging one is always a wise decision. A real estate attorney in Jacksonville, FL, provides a layer of protection that a real estate agent or title company cannot offer. Agents facilitate the deal, and title companies ensure the title is clear, but only an attorney can provide legal advice and represent your exclusive interests. Consider hiring a real estate attorney in these common scenarios:

  • You are buying or selling property, especially high-value or commercial real estate.
  • The transaction involves complex financing or a non-traditional sale structure.
  • You are purchasing property from out-of-state.
  • The property has known issues, such as unpermitted work or potential title defects.
  • You are dealing with a foreclosure, short sale, or estate sale.
  • You are entering into a commercial lease agreement.
  • You are involved in a dispute with a co-owner, neighbor, contractor, or tenant.

In each of these situations, the guidance of a real estate lawyer in Jacksonville, FL, can help you avoid costly mistakes and navigate legal challenges with confidence.

Why Should a Real Estate Attorney Review the Contracts?

Real estate contracts are complex legal documents filled with jargon and critical deadlines. A skilled attorney will scrutinize the contract to ensure the terms are fair, identify unfavorable clauses, and confirm that all necessary contingencies (like financing and inspections) are included to protect you. We make sure you fully understand your obligations and rights before you sign, preventing misunderstandings and future litigation.
