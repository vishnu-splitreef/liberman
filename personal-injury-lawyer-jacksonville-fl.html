Cases a Jacksonville Personal Injury Attorney Handles Often Involve:

Our team at Lieberman Chamberlain PLLC has the breadth of experience to manage a wide array of personal injury and complex litigation cases. If your injury was caused by negligence, we are equipped to help. Our practice areas include:

Car Accidents

Accidents involving passenger vehicles, including rear-end, T-bone, and head-on collisions caused by careless or distracted driving.

Truck Accidents

High-stakes cases involving commercial semi-trucks, 18-wheelers, and tractor-trailers, often involving federal regulations.

Scooter Accident

Injuries resulting from rental services like Lime/Bird scooter accidents due to defective equipment, poorly maintained paths, or negligent riders.

Rideshare Accident

Claims involving Uber & Lyft Accidents, addressing liability of the driver, the rideshare company, and other involved parties.

Bus Accident

Injuries sustained as a passenger, pedestrian, or driver involved in a collision with a municipal, school, or commercial bus.

Distracted Driving Accidents

Cases where injuries result from a driver using a phone, texting, or engaging in other activities that take their attention from the road.

Pedestrian Accident

Serious injuries to walkers and joggers hit by negligent drivers.

Motorcycle Accidents

High-impact collisions leading to severe injuries for riders due to the lack of physical protection.

Slip and Fall Accidents / Premises Liability

Injuries caused by dangerous conditions on someone else’s property, such as wet floors, broken stairs, or poor lighting.

Catastrophic Injuries

Claims involving life-altering harm with long-term consequences, including:

  • Brain Injury (Traumatic Brain Injury or TBI)
  • Spinal Injury/Back Injury (Paralysis, paraplegia, quadriplegia)
  • Birth Injury (Including Cerebral Palsy caused by medical negligence)

Medical Malpractice

Injuries or death resulting from negligent care by a healthcare professional, including surgical errors, misdiagnosis, or birth complications.

Workplace Accidents / Construction Accident

Injuries sustained on the job, requiring coordination between workers’ compensation claims and third-party liability claims.

Wrongful Death

Legal actions brought by the family of a person killed due to negligence, seeking compensation for their loss, pain, and financial support.

Frequently Asked Questions About Personal Injury Law

Q.01Will I Get More Settlement Money With a Personal Injury Lawyer?

Statistically, yes. Studies and industry experience consistently show that victims represented by a qualified personal injury lawyer Jacksonville FL recover significantly more compensation than those who try to handle their claims alone—even after accounting for the attorney’s fee. An attorney ensures all future costs are accounted for, handles complex negotiations, and is not intimidated by insurance company tactics.

Q.02Does My Personal Injury Case Have to go to Trial?

No, the vast majority of personal injury cases (over 95%) settle before ever reaching a courtroom trial. However, preparing a case for trial is the most effective way to achieve a favorable settlement. The insurance company knows which firms are willing to litigate and which are not. At Lieberman Chamberlain PLLC, our reputation as skilled trial attorneys is a significant advantage in the settlement process.

Q.03Do I Need a Personal Injury Lawyer?

If your injury is minor and fully covered by insurance with no lasting effects, you might not need one. However, if you have sustained a serious injury, missed significant time from work, or been offered a low settlement by the insurance company, you absolutely need an experienced Jacksonville personal injury lawyer. The insurance company’s job is to minimize its payout; our job is to maximize your recovery.

Q.04How Do I Afford a Personal Injury Lawyer in Jacksonville?

You can afford the best legal representation through our contingency fee agreement. You are never asked to pay upfront costs or hourly fees. Our firm fronts all the necessary expenses for litigation, and we only recoup those costs and our fee if we successfully win your case. This ensures everyone in Jacksonville, regardless of their financial situation, has access to premier legal advocacy.
