§ 01 Biography

Counsel shaped by experience.

Christopher D. Chamberlain is a Florida attorney with more than a decade of legal experience, he advises clients on real estate matters, and title issues, delivering practical solutions with a strong focus on efficiency and client service.

Mr. Chamberlain earned his Juris Doctor from the University of Florida Fredric G. Levin College of Law and was admitted to The Florida Bar in 2014. He is an active member of several Florida Bar sections, including Real Property, Probate & Trust Law; Elder Law; and Entertainment, Arts & Sports Law.

Prior to being a Partner at Lieberman Chamberlain, PLLC, Mr. Chamberlain served as Senior Associate at The Post Law Firm, P.A., and practiced at Cole, Scott & Kissane, P.A., where he handled litigation, and business matters. He also served as Claims Counsel and Assistant Vice President at Fidelity National Financial, managing complex title insurance claims and litigation nationwide.

Mr. Chamberlain is known for his responsive approach, professionalism, and commitment to helping clients navigate real estate transactions with clarity and confidence.
