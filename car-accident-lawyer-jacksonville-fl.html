Immediate Steps to Take After a Car Accident in Jacksonville

The actions you take following a car accident are crucial. They can protect your health and form the foundation of a strong legal claim.

Ensure Safety

If possible, move your vehicle to a safe location off the main road to prevent further accidents. Turn on your hazard lights.

Check for Injuries and Call 911

The health of everyone involved is the top priority. Check on your passengers and the occupants of the other vehicle(s). Call 911 immediately to report the crash and request medical assistance for anyone who is injured. A police report is a vital piece of evidence for your claim.

Seek Medical Attention

Even if you feel fine, it’s essential to be evaluated by a medical professional. Adrenaline can mask pain, and some serious injuries, like whiplash or internal bleeding, may not have immediate symptoms.

Exchange Information

Get the name, contact information, driver’s license number, and insurance details from all other drivers involved. Also, get the license plate numbers and the make and model of their vehicles.

Document the Scene

Use your phone to take pictures and videos of the accident scene from multiple angles. Capture the damage to all vehicles, skid marks on the road, traffic signals, weather conditions, and any visible injuries you have sustained.

Speak to Witnesses

If there were any witnesses, ask for their names and contact information. Their testimony can be invaluable in proving who was at fault.

Do Not Admit Fault

Be polite when interacting with other drivers, but do not apologize or admit fault for the accident. Any such statements can be used against you by the insurance company.

Contact an Attorney

Before you speak with an insurance adjuster, consult with a legal professional. An experienced car accident attorney in Jacksonville, FL, can advise you on your rights and handle all communications with the insurance companies on your behalf.

The Most Critical Question: Do I Really Need a Jacksonville Car Accident Lawyer?

After a minor fender-bender with no injuries, you might be able to handle the property damage claim yourself. But if you were injured—even if you think it’s “just whiplash”—hiring an experienced car accident attorney is not a luxury; it is a necessity.

Here’s why:

The insurance adjuster for the other driver is not your friend. They are a trained negotiator whose job is to minimize their company’s liability. They will call you immediately, acting with fake sympathy, and try to:

Get a Recorded Statement

They will ask you trick questions to get you to admit partial fault or downplay your injuries. (“You were only going 5 mph over the limit?” or “You’re feeling a bit better today, right?”). This recording will be used against you.

Get You to Sign a Quick Release

They will offer you a fast check for $1,000 or $2,000. This is a trap. If you accept it, you sign away your right to ever seek more money, even if you later discover your “minor” back pain is a herniated disc requiring surgery.

Dig Through Your Medical History

They will ask for a blanket medical authorization to find a “pre-existing condition” they can use to deny your claim.

As your  attorney, we stop this immediately. We send a formal letter of representation to all parties. From that moment on, all communication goes through us. The phone calls stop. The harassment ends. We become your shield.

Florida’s Statute of Limitations

It is critical to act quickly after a car accident. Florida has a strict deadline, known as the statute of limitations, for filing a personal injury lawsuit. For most car accident cases, you generally have only two years from the date of the accident to file your claim. If you miss this deadline, you will likely lose your right to seek compensation forever.

While two years may seem like a long time, building a strong case takes considerable effort. Evidence can disappear, witnesses’ memories fade, and investigations take time. The sooner you contact an attorney the stronger your position will be.
