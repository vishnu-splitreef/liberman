Commercial Lending Services in Jacksonville

Our firm offers comprehensive services across the entire life cycle of a commercial financing transaction, from initial structuring to closing and post-closing matters.

Real Estate Acquisition, Construction Financing, and Permanent Facilities

We represent clients in financing transactions for the acquisition, development, and construction of commercial real estate projects, including office buildings, retail centers, industrial properties, and multi-family housing. Our services cover everything from initial loan commitment to the closing of permanent financing.

Asset-Based Loans (Term and Revolving Facilities)

Our team is highly skilled in structuring asset-based lending facilities, where loans are secured by a company’s assets, such as accounts receivable, inventory, and equipment. We handle both term loans and revolving lines of credit, creating flexible financing solutions that support our clients’ operational needs.

Timeshare Receivables and Resort Development Financing

The hospitality industry has unique financing needs. We have specialized expertise in structuring loans secured by timeshare receivables and financing for the development and expansion of resorts and other vacation properties.

Leveraged Buyouts and Mergers & Acquisitions (Stock and Asset Transactions)

We provide critical legal support for acquisition financing, including leveraged buyouts and other M&A activities. Our team assists with structuring the debt financing for both stock and asset purchases, ensuring the deal is legally sound and financially viable.

Equipment Finance

For businesses that rely on heavy machinery or specialized technology, equipment financing is essential. We assist both lenders and borrowers in structuring loans and leases for the acquisition of critical business equipment.

Inter-Creditor and Subordination Agreements

In transactions involving multiple lenders, clearly defining the priority of debts is crucial. We are experts at drafting and negotiating inter-creditor and subordination agreements that protect our clients’ positions and clarify rights in the event of a default.

Letters of Credit, Loan Syndications, Participations, and Interest-Rate Swaps

Our expertise extends to more sophisticated financial instruments. We provide counsel on letters of credit, syndicated loans where multiple lenders fund a single credit facility, loan participations, and interest-rate swap agreements used to manage risk.

How a Commercial Lending Lawyer Can Help Your Business

A commercial lending lawyer plays a critical role beyond simply drafting documents. They act as a strategic advisor, helping to structure the transaction in the most advantageous way, anticipate potential problems, and protect your interests from start to finish.

An experienced commercial lending lawyer in Jacksonville, FL, will:

  • Structure the Deal: Assist in determining the optimal loan structure to meet the borrower’s needs while protecting the lender’s investment.
  • Conduct Due diligence: Meticulously review all relevant financial documents, corporate records, and collateral to identify any potential legal risks or liabilities.
  • Draft and Negotiate Documents: Prepare and negotiate all necessary legal documentation, including loan agreements, security agreements, promissory notes, and guarantees, ensuring the terms are clear, fair, and enforceable.
  • Ensure Regulatory Compliance: Navigate the complex web of state and federal regulations that govern lending to ensure the transaction is fully compliant.
  • Facilitate a Smooth Closing: Coordinate all aspects of the closing process to ensure all conditions are met and the transaction is completed efficiently.
