Commercial evictions are not the same as residential ones. The procedural requirements are complex, “self-help” lockouts are illegal and dangerous, and the financial stakes are significantly higher. A single procedural misstep can set your case back by weeks—or get it dismissed entirely. At Lieberman Chamberlain PLLC, our commercial eviction lawyers in Jacksonville, FL know exactly how to navigate Florida’s complex commercial landlord-tenant laws to minimize your losses.

02

The Critical Risk of “Self-Help” Evictions

When a commercial tenant stops paying rent, a landlord’s immediate instinct is often to change the locks, shut off utilities, or physically bar entry to minimize further losses. In Florida, this is known as a “self-help” eviction, and it is strictly illegal.

Under Florida law, regardless of how many months a tenant is behind on rent or how egregious their lease violations are, a landlord must utilize the formal court process to regain possession. Attempting to bypass the courts can instantly flip the legal script, exposing you to severe liabilities, including statutory damages, tenant attorney’s fees, and claims of unlawful conversion or trespass. Retaining a dedicated property eviction lawyer in jacksonville fl ensures that your real estate asset is recovered legally and safely, completely eliminating the risk of costly tenant counter-lawsuits.

How the commercial eviction process works

1. Review your lease and grounds for eviction

We start by thoroughly reviewing your commercial lease agreement and the circumstances of the dispute to confirm you have valid legal grounds to proceed.

2. Serve the required legal notice

Florida law requires specific written notices before filing for eviction. We prepare and serve the correct notice—whether that is a 3-day notice for non-payment or another form—ensuring full legal compliance from the outset.

3. File the eviction action

If the tenant fails to comply with the notice, we file the eviction complaint with the appropriate Jacksonville court and manage all required documentation on your behalf.

4. Attend hearings and pursue judgment

Our attorneys represent you at all court hearings, advocating firmly for your right to recover possession of your commercial property and vigorously defending against tenant counterclaims.

5. Recover your property

Once judgment is entered in your favor, we help coordinate the final steps to restore your full control over the property, including obtaining a Writ of Possession and pursuing damages for unpaid rent and costs.

05

Beyond Possession: Landlord Liens and Rent Acceleration

A successful commercial property eviction in jacksonville fl shouldn’t just hand you back the keys to an empty building—it should actively work to recover the money you are owed. When managing a vacancy, we look beyond physical possession to deploy advanced legal mechanisms designed to salvage your bottom line:

Statutory Landlord Liens

Under Florida Statute § 83.08, commercial landlords automatically hold a lien for unpaid rent upon all personal property, equipment, and inventory belonging to the tenant located on the leased premises. A strategic jacksonville commercial eviction attorney can initiate “distress for rent” proceedings to legally levy and freeze these assets before the tenant can covertly move them off-site.

Lease Acceleration Remedies

If your commercial lease agreement contains an acceleration clause, you may have the legal right to demand the immediate payment of all remaining rent due through the entire life of the lease term, rather than just the past-due balance.

Our legal team thoroughly reviews your contract to ensure every tool—from seizing on-site equipment to accelerating future lease payments—is utilized to maximize your financial recovery.

Neutralizing the Threat of Tenant Bankruptcy

For commercial property owners, the ultimate roadblock is a non-paying tenant filing for bankruptcy right in the middle of an eviction proceeding. The moment a tenant files a bankruptcy petition, a federal “automatic stay” goes into effect. This instantly freezes all state court eviction lawsuits, and attempting to move forward with the eviction without explicit federal permission can result in crippling court sanctions.

When a tenant attempts to weaponize the bankruptcy code to stall for time, a skilled commercial eviction lawyer in jacksonville fl can immediately pivot into federal court to protect your asset. We frequently represent landlords in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Middle District of Florida to file Motions for Relief from the Automatic Stay. This allows us to either lift the federal freeze so your commercial property eviction in jacksonville fl can cross the finish line, or force the tenant to immediately begin paying current “administrative rent” for every day they continue to occupy your commercial space.

Frequently asked questions

Q.01What are the grounds for a commercial eviction in Florida?

Common grounds include non-payment of rent, violation of lease terms, expiration of the lease, or unauthorized use of the property. Florida law requires that specific notice procedures be followed before any eviction action can be filed in court.

Q.02How long does a commercial eviction take in Jacksonville, FL?

The timeline varies depending on the complexity of the case and whether the tenant contests the eviction. An uncontested eviction can often be resolved in a matter of weeks. Our attorneys work to move your case forward as efficiently as possible.

Q.03Can a tenant fight a commercial eviction?

Yes. A tenant may raise defenses such as improper notice, lease disputes, or claims that the landlord failed to meet their own obligations. Having an experienced commercial eviction attorney on your side ensures that any defenses are properly and promptly addressed.

Q.04What if my tenant refuses to leave after a court order?

If a tenant remains on the property after a court judgment, a Writ of Possession can be obtained, authorizing law enforcement to remove the tenant. We handle this process on your behalf.

Q.05Does Lieberman Chamberlain PLLC handle other commercial property matters?

Yes. In addition to commercial property eviction in Jacksonville, FL, our firm handles a broad range of real estate law matters, including lease disputes, title issues, and commercial lending transactions.
